Case study title

One paragraph that states the problem, the move you made, and the outcome. Someone who reads only this should know whether to keep reading.

Context

What was the situation before you got involved. What did the product look like, who was it for, what was the org around it.

Keep it to two or three paragraphs. This section is setup, not story.

Problem

State the problem as a decision that had to be made, not as a gap in the feature list. What was genuinely at stake and what made it hard.

  • Constraint one, e.g. a fixed GA date.
  • Constraint two, e.g. an enterprise customer blocking on it.
  • Constraint three, e.g. no historical usage data to learn from.

The decision

What you chose, stated plainly in one sentence. Then the alternatives you rejected and why.

This is the most valuable part of the page. Anyone can describe what shipped. Very few write down what they chose not to do.

What I did

Your specific actions, not the team's. Conversations, documents, tradeoffs, sequencing.

  • Action one and what it changed.
  • Action two and what it changed.
  • Action three and what it changed.

Result

What happened, with numbers. Distinguish between what you measured and what you estimated.

If it only partly worked, say so. Credibility is worth more than a clean ending.

What I'd change

One or two things you would do differently. This section is optional, but it is the one that reads as senior.
